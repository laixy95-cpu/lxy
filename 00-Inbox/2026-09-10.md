## Findings that change the paper

1. **A1 — the abstract's central claim is false.** "The only regional estimate
   whose cross-country interval excludes zero" — energy-service/COVID
   (−0.077, CI [−0.125, −0.029]) also excludes zero *and* also sits below every
   placebo window. Appears in 4 places. The defensible version already exists in
   the paper: macro-price/compound has the largest gap-to-RMSE ratio (0.86) of
   all six cells.
2. **A17 — §2.4 contradicts §5.1, §5.2 and Table 7 on Singapore.** §2.4 says all
   six economies administer prices; everywhere else says Singapore does not. §5.1
   explains Singapore's −1.816 *by* that absence, so this is load-bearing.
3. **NV1 — §5.4's Vietnam evidence argues against §5.4's thesis.** Decision
   13/2020 was issued 6 April 2020, inside the COVID stage; ~6 GW of ~9.3 GW of
   2020 rooftop solar was commissioned in December 2020 racing its deadline. The
   capacity is attributed to the 2017–2019 FIT programme. And Vietnam is the
   country whose removal flips the headline sign.
4. **A4 — a promised robustness check is never reported.** §3.6 lists seven;
   Table 8 has six. The missing one — uniform linear form — tests the paper's
   central methodological choice.
5. **A5 — the gap-to-RMSE denominator is underspecified.** Printed ratios
   reproduce only as the mean of two horizon RMSEs (6/6), never a single horizon
   (0/6). This ratio now carries the headline claim.
6. **NV2 — does `RenCap` include large hydro?** Vietnam's stated 268→391 W/person
   only reconciles if it does. If so, the indicator is partly legacy stock, which
   affects §4.3's headline and Table 3's log-linear justification.

## The big finding: the code doesn't match the manuscript

1. **The script's own recorded conclusion is the opposite of the paper's headline.**
   `Results_Interpretation_CN.md` (hardcoded at line 356) says macro-price pressure
   was **not** the 2022–23 shortfall — its regional gap was **positive** — and
   low-carbon continuity had the **largest negative** gap. The manuscript says
   macro-price −0.767 (the shortfall) and low-carbon +0.102 (positive).
2. **The counterfactual is uniform linear on dimension scores** — `np.polyfit(…, 1)`
   on `train[dim]`. No logit, no log-linear, no pre-crisis mean, no indicator-level
   fitting. That is exactly what §3.4 calls "inadmissible for the log and logit forms".
   **Table 3 has no implementation.**
3. **Five reported tables have no code path**: Table 3, Table 4's intervals and
   ratios, Table 5, Table 6, Table 9, plus Figs. 3–4. Zero grep matches for
   `rmse|placebo|rolling|backtest|interval`.
4. **It explains A4.** The uniform-linear "robustness check" the manuscript promises
   but never reports *is* this script's main specification.

Most likely benign: this is an earlier revision, and the "counterfactual
specification module" named in Data Availability wasn't uploaded. But until it
surfaces, none of the headline numbers is reproducible.

answers R2#1 (the deepest objection); Table 1 was built for R2#2 and R4#3; removing
every regression answers R2#5, R2#6 and R4#4.

Dropping the intervals and leading with gap-to-RMSE resolves A1, A11, R4 and R2#6
in a single move.
